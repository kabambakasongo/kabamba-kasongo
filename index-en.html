01 — About

ERP trainer and full-stack developer, serving skills and concrete solutions

I am KABAMBA KASONGO, an ERP trainer and full-stack developer based in Kolwezi, Democratic Republic of the Congo. I train professionals and teams on ERP systems — SAP S/4HANA and all its modules, Odoo, Sage, Microsoft Dynamics — as well as office tools, payroll calculation and labour law.

I also design and develop web applications, SaaS platforms and management software that address real needs, turning ideas and business requirements into modern, high-performing and easy-to-use digital solutions.

My work covers the whole chain: architecture design, frontend and backend development, databases, automation and deployment — with the same commitment to knowledge sharing and documentation.
